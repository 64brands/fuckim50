Privacy policy

The I’M 50! Show is a podcast hosted by Paul Burfitt and run by P1 Shootout Pty Ltd trading as 64 Brands (ABN 87 683 900 183) from Airlie Beach, Queensland. In this policy, “we” and “us” means us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • When you use the contact or guest form: your name, email address, phone number and whatever you tell us in your message.
  • If you come on the show: your recording, and anything you share in the conversation.
  • Visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t follow you around other websites. Our hosting provider also logs basic technical details, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure.

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, organise and publish guest episodes, look at partnership enquiries, and run and improve the show. We won’t add you to a mailing list or send you marketing without your permission.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We use a few services to run the site and our email:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and counts visits.
  • Resend delivers form messages to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace runs our email.

These providers may store information overseas, including in the United States. We’ll also share information if the law requires it.

Players and links

Episode pages include the Acast audio player, and we link to Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Those services have their own privacy policies and may use cookies when you play an episode or follow a link.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and only keep it for as long as we need it. We don’t use automated decision-making.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

Get in touch through our contact page and we’ll sort it out. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, contact us first through our contact page and we’ll respond within 30 days. If it’s still not sorted, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
