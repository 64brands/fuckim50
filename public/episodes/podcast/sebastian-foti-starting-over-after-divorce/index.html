Starting Over After Divorce: Sebastian Foti on Fatherhood, Career Change & Midlife

After more than 20 years of marriage, Sebastian Foti found himself navigating divorce, raising three teenage children and rebuilding his life as he approached 50.

21 Oct 202631 min

Listen to the episode

About this episode

After more than 20 years of marriage, Sebastian Foti found himself navigating divorce, raising three teenage children and working out what the next part of life should look like as he headed towards 50.

In this episode of FI50 — F*CK! I’M 50, Paul Burfitt sits down with his longtime mate on the waterfront in the Whitsundays, Queensland, for a candid conversation about starting over after divorce, fatherhood, friendship, career change and finding a better balance between work and life.

A mechanical engineer who has spent much of his career in mining, Sebastian talks openly about the end of his marriage, becoming a single parent, the importance of having people to talk to and why honest advice from good friends helped him through a difficult period.

They also talk about Sebastian’s decision to leave a demanding job filled with long hours and work travel for an even-time remote mining roster — giving him more genuine time with his children and more space for the things he enjoys.

Cars are a big part of that life. Sebastian discusses restoring and building classic Fords, his involvement with the local Whitsundays car community and the annual car show at Coral Sea Marina in Airlie Beach, as well as why working on cars has become both a passion and a useful escape from everyday stress.

The conversation moves through investing and financial regrets, parenting teenagers, being more present with family, redefining success in midlife, qigong meditation, salsa dancing and the value of deliberately doing things outside your comfort zone.

Most importantly, Sebastian comes back to one simple lesson from his own experience: if you’re struggling, talk to someone — and if you see someone else struggling, take the time to listen.

A genuine conversation about divorce, midlife change, fatherhood, work-life balance, friendship and discovering that starting again doesn’t mean starting from nothing.

This is FI50 — F*CK! I’M 50.
