The I’M 50! Show with Paul Burfitt

One minute you’re busy working, raising kids and dealing with life. The next you wake up and realise — I’M 50! The kids hardly need you anymore, and your parents need you more than ever.

The I’M 50! Show is an Australian podcast about this stage of life. Each episode tackles a different topic — family, money, health, work, relationships, ageing parents, regrets and starting again — through solo episodes and honest conversations with everyday people and a few faces you might know.

I’m Paul Burfitt, a bloke from Queensland. Not a guru, not a life coach, just someone who’s decided to stop putting things off. More about the show
