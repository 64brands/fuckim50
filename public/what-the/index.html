F*CK! I’M 50 started with a pretty simple question: where the bloody hell did the last 20 years go?

One minute you’re busy working, raising kids and dealing with life, the next you wake up and realise you’re Fuck! I'm 50!

My name's Paul Burfitt. My mates call me Burf.

I’m not a guru, a life coach or an expert. I’m just a bloke from Queensland who’s decided I’m not going to keep putting everything off.

So I’m talking to people from around Australia and around the world about the stuff that actually comes up at this stage of life — family, money, health, work, relationships, ambitions, regrets, fuck-ups, starting again and figuring out what makes you happy.

Some conversations will be with everyday people who have great stories. Others might be with people you already know from sport, media, business or somewhere completely unexpected. And every now and then I’ll bring in someone who genuinely knows what they’re talking about when we get into things like health or money.

We've got a podcast and an little youTube channel set up and I'm going to bring you along for the ride. I’m going to start ticking off a few things I’ve spent years saying I’d do “one day”.

I'm sure some will think it's a brilliantly idea. Plenty probably won’t. But either way here we go.

There's sure to be some spills, thrills and the occasional disaster as I try to make up for a bit of lost time.

F*CK! I’M 50 isn’t about having all the answers. It’s about finally asking the questions — and doing something about them.
